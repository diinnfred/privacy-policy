Most basic x1live features can be accessed without creating an account. However, certain features, including personalized features, profile functionality, favorites, community features, and other account-based services, may require you to sign in.

Depending on the features you choose to use, we may process certain information, including:

  • Account information such as your name and email address when using supported authentication services.

We use this information only for legitimate app functionality, authentication, security, personalization, support, and service improvement.

x1live may request certain device permissions when they are necessary for a specific feature.

  • Camera: May be requested when you choose to capture a new profile image.

Permissions are requested only when required by the relevant functionality. You can manage or revoke app permissions at any time through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from functioning correctly.

x1live may provide third-party authentication methods such as Google Sign-In or other supported account providers.

When you use a third-party authentication provider, certain account information may be provided to us according to the permissions you approve and the privacy policies of that provider.

x1live may use third-party services to provide authentication, notifications, analytics, app functionality, and performance monitoring.

Users who have created an account may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.
